# Question - Initial Supported Formats

> [!summary]
> **Status:** Open
> **Product:** [[Product - Overview]]

## Context

The initial problem is a very large PDF, but the product direction is explicitly book-centric rather than PDF-centric. Other book/document formats should be supported eventually.

## Why It Matters

The first format set affects the initial product scope and the portability of reading concepts such as progress and location.

## Options / Considerations

- PDF is the initial concrete use case.
- EPUB and other formats are future possibilities.

## Current Answer

Not decided. PDF is the initial problem space, but the product should not be architecturally or conceptually limited to PDF.
