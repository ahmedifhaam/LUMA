# Question - Initial Product and Market Positioning

> [!summary]
> **Status:** Open
> **Product:** [[Product - Overview]]

## Context

The discussion identified that a generic book library has many established competitors and therefore is not sufficient differentiation. The current product thesis combines high-performance reading of difficult/large documents with a longer-term reading-to-knowledge experience.

## Why It Matters

The initial target user and the strongest willingness-to-pay problem should determine what is built first and how the product is positioned.

## Options / Considerations

Potential initial audiences discussed include:

- Serious readers.
- Technical/academic readers.
- Knowledge workers.
- Students and researchers.

Potential positioning directions include:

- High-performance reader for very large documents.
- Local-first serious reading platform.
- Reading and personal knowledge platform.

## Current Answer

Not decided. The current strongest product direction is a personal reading and knowledge platform, with very large-document performance as an initial wedge.
