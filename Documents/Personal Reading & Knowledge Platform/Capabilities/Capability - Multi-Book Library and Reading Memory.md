# Capability - Multi-Book Library and Reading Memory

> [!summary]
> **Status:** Explored
> **Product:** [[Product - Overview]]
> **Phase:** [[Phase 1 - Local Reading Foundation]]

## Purpose

Provide a book-centric library that manages multiple books and preserves the user's relationship with each book beyond the currently open page.

## Description

The product should feel more like a personal reading library than a standalone PDF viewer. Books should have persistent reading progress and history, allowing users to return to unfinished books and continue from meaningful reading positions.

The desired experience is comparable in spirit to a modern book library such as Apple Books, but with a stronger emphasis on serious reading, large documents, annotations, and eventual knowledge building.

## Why It Matters

A generic book library is not sufficient differentiation by itself. Its role is to provide the foundation around which the differentiated reading and knowledge experience is organized.

## Current Understanding

Potentially useful reading memory includes:

- Current reading position.
- Progress through a book.
- Last-read information.
- Reading history.
- Bookmarks and highlights.
- Notes.

The exact reading statistics and presentation are not yet specified.

## Scope

### In Scope / Emerging

- Multiple books.
- Persistent progress.
- Continue-reading experience.
- Bookmarks and highlights.
- Reading history.

### Deferred

- Advanced reading analytics.
- AI-generated reading guidance.
- Cross-book knowledge relationships.
