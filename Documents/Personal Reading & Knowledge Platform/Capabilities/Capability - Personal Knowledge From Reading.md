# Capability - Personal Knowledge From Reading

> [!summary]
> **Status:** Candidate
> **Product:** [[Product - Overview]]
> **Phase:** Not Assigned

## Purpose

Turn reading activity into durable personal knowledge rather than treating books as content that is only consumed and forgotten.

## Description

The longer-term product direction is to connect what a user highlights, notes, learns, and revisits across multiple books. Books act as sources; annotations and reading activity become part of the user's personal knowledge context.

Potential future experiences include understanding selected passages, connecting related concepts across books, and helping the user resume not only a page but the surrounding understanding.

## Why It Matters

A generic library or AI-enabled PDF viewer is difficult to differentiate. The emerging product thesis is that the deeper value comes from helping users **read, understand, remember, and connect** information over time.

## Current Understanding

Possible areas discussed:

- Highlights and notes as durable knowledge.
- Cross-book relationships.
- Context-aware assistance while reading.
- AI-assisted explanation or simplification.
- Returning to previous notes and concepts.
- A future personal knowledge graph.

These are candidate directions, not current requirements.

## Scope

### Future Candidate

- Personal knowledge derived from reading.
- Cross-book concept connections.
- AI assistance for understanding.
- Reading memory that includes prior context, not just page position.

### Explicitly Not Current Scope

The knowledge layer should not be allowed to inflate the initial reader into a complete general-purpose knowledge-management product before the core reading experience is validated.

## Alternatives

A generic AI chat interface over a PDF was discussed implicitly as an insufficient differentiator. AI should be a capability within the reading experience rather than the sole identity of the product.
